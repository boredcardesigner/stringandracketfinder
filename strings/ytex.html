YTEX tennis strings compared

Every YTEX string bed on the bench — 34 setups across the brand's 17 strings, rated on eight axes including arm friendliness. YTEX publishes no stiffness figures, so these ratings are derived from material, profile and gauge and are marked as estimates throughout.

How the range scores

Across these 34 beds the range averages 8.1 for spin and 5.7 for arm friendliness. Its strongest axes are spin (8.1) and precision (7.9); its weakest is arm friendliness (5.7). 17 of the 34 are hybrids rather than full beds. Gauges represented: 1.15 · 1.20 · 1.23 · 1.25 · 1.26 · 1.27 · 1.28 · 1.30 · 1.32 · 1.34 mm.

Every rating on this page is derived, not measured. YTEX publishes the material, profile and gauge of its range but no stiffness, tension-loss or energy-return figure for any string in it. Those columns are therefore estimated by class against strings already rated on this bench, and every YTEX bed carries an est. marker in the tool. Where a bed lands on stats identical to a string already on the bench it carries a twin marker naming that string, rather than implying a difference the published data does not support — which for a player weighing a cheaper brand is the useful answer.

Matching a string to your racket

A string only makes sense in a frame. A stiff polyester in an already stiff, powerful racket is the most common cause of arm trouble in club tennis, and the same string in a flexible control frame can be excellent. The finder scores the pairing rather than the string alone.
