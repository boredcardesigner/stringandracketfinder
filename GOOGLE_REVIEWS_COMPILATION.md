## SUMMARY: WHAT DRIVES TRUST

1. **Transparency** — Reasoning visible, not hidden
2. **Physics-based** — Lab data, not crowdsourced reviews
3. **No commercial influence** — Brands don't pay to appear higher
4. **Personalization** — Recommendations change based on *your* priorities, not category averages
5. **Surprise value** — Customers discover strings they wouldn't have searched for
6. **Honest about limitations** — Estimated scores marked as estimates; a 10 requires court play
