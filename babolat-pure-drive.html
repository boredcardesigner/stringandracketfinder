Model notes

Pure Drive 2015

The power benchmark, and still one of the stiffest frames in wide use. Pace is genuinely on tap — depth requires very little from you and serves come off it fast. That stiffness is the whole story though: it can be unforgiving on the arm and control is conditional on taming it with soft strings and real racquet-head speed. Enormously effective when it fits, punishing when it does not.

Pure Drive 98 (2021)

A Pure Drive with the head and pattern pulled in — the free power is still there but with a lower launch and meaningfully tighter dispersion than the 100. It remains a stiff frame with a firm, energetic response, so string choice matters for the arm. Best read as the Pure Drive for someone who liked the pace but kept hitting long.

Choosing between them

Frames in one family share a construction story but not a playing character — head size, pattern and swingweight move the numbers above far more than the marketing does.
