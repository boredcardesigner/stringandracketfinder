Model notes

Pro Staff X v14

The 100-square-inch Pro Staff, at around 315g. It keeps the line's control bias and muted feel but adds a usefully larger sweet spot and a touch more launch, which makes it far less punishing on off-centre contact. A sensible middle ground for a player who wants Pro Staff precision without committing to a 97 head and the timing discipline that demands.

Pro Staff 97 v14

The modern Pro Staff: 97 square inches at around 315g, control-first, with the muted, connected feel the line is known for. It rewards full swings and clean contact with excellent precision and a very stable, planted response. Do not expect free depth — this is a frame you drive, not one that drives for you. Comfortable enough for long play, unlike the stiffer control frames of a decade ago.

Pro Staff 97 v13

The previous-generation 97, and marginally the firmer, more muted of the two. Same fundamental proposition — control, stability, a very direct response on flat balls — with slightly less of the softened impact Wilson dialled into the following version. If you prefer your feedback blunt and your launch low, the older frame arguably makes the point more clearly.

Choosing between them

Frames in one family share a construction story but not a playing character — head size, pattern and swingweight move the numbers above far more than the marketing does.
