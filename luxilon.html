String notes

LUXILON 4G

Built specifically for tension maintenance — it holds its stringbed longer than almost any other polyester, which makes it a strong choice for frequent players who dislike the way poly goes dead. Firm and controlled, with a slightly muted response. Expect consistency over excitement: it plays much the same in week three as in week one.

LUXILON 4G

LUXILON ALU Power 1.25

The tour standard for a reason. Crisp, precise and beautifully connected — it gives you an unusually clear sense of where the ball is on the bed. Control and predictability are outstanding. It is stiff and it loses tension noticeably as it ages, so its best window is short. Play it fresh, or hybrid it if you want the feel without the full firmness.

LUXILON ALU Power Rough 1.25

The textured ALU Power. The rougher surface adds noticeable spin bite over the smooth version at a small cost in comfort and playing life. Same crisp, precise character underneath. Choose it over standard ALU if you want more grab on the ball and are willing to cut the string a little sooner.

LUXILON 4G 1.25

Built specifically for tension maintenance — it holds its stringbed longer than almost any other polyester, which makes it a strong choice for frequent players who dislike the way poly goes dead. Firm and controlled, with a slightly muted response. Expect consistency over excitement: it plays much the same in week three as in week one.

LUXILON 4G Soft 1.25

The gentler 4G. It keeps the family's excellent tension stability but drops the stiffness meaningfully, so the impact is more comfortable and the ball pockets a little longer. A good compromise for a player who wants poly control and durability but finds a full firm bed hard on the arm.

Matching a string to your racket

A string only makes sense in a frame. A stiff polyester in an already stiff, powerful racket is the most common cause of arm trouble in club tennis, and the same string in a flexible control frame can be excellent. The finder scores the pairing rather than the string alone.
